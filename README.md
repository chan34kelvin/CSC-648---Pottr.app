# CSC 648 Pottr.app
Pottr is a novel video monetization platform that offers 
low-risk video game clip based competitions. 

● We target the gamers, people who enjoy playing video games and want to post 
their cool video game clips 

● Gamers from content creators to the average college student who like to play 
in their free time

● An example of our customer would be your average gamer Joe, who just likes 
to play games on his free time

[🦦✨Pottr✨🦦](https://pottr.app/) *Currently not working*
